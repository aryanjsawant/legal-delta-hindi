Instead of training a separate "Reward Model" to judge the LLM, DPO uses a dataset of pairs: a Preferred response and a Rejected response.

It forces the model to increase the probability of the preferred answer while decreasing the probability of the rejected one.\nhttps://www.overleaf.com/1422428328nrxrxjrwswdv#19f666\n\n--- Slide 5 ---\n\nGRPO: Group Relative Policy Optimization\nIt generates a group of multiple outputs for the same prompt and rewards the ones that perform best relative to the others. 

It uses "Reward Functions" (code-based rules) to score outputs.

GRPO eliminates the need for a massive "Critic" model, saving huge amounts of VRAM, which is why it works so well with libraries like Unsloth.\n\n--- Slide 6 ---\n\n\nDPO on il_tur dataset
